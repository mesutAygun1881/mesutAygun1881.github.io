Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 06, 2026

CineSpace ("the App") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you use the CineSpace mobile application, a movie-themed social discovery and dating platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide meaningful connections based on cinematic tastes:

A. Information You Provide:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, age, gender, and school/job information provided during registration or profile setup.
  • Profile Content: Your profile photos ("Cast Photos") and bio.
  • Cinematic Preferences: Movies, TV shows, and actors you select as favorites via our integration with The Movie Database (TMDB).
  • User Content (UGC): Messages sent to other users via the chat feature and interactions such as "Superlikes".

B. Information Collected Automatically:

  • Usage Data: Analytics regarding your interactions within the App (e.g., profiles viewed, screens visited, features used) via Google Firebase Analytics.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers (UUID).
  • Purchase History: Information regarding subscriptions (monthly, annual) and consumable purchases (Superlikes) processed via RevenueCat. Note: We do not store your raw credit card information; transactions are handled by Apple's App Store.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Matching Algorithm: To suggest potential matches based on shared movie/TV show interests and demographic preferences.
  • Communication: To facilitate secure messaging between matched users.
  • Transaction Processing: To manage your Premium subscriptions and Consumable items (Superlikes).
  • Safety & Security: To detect and prevent abuse, spam, or content that violates our community guidelines.
  • App Improvements: To fix bugs and analyze performance (via Firebase Crashlytics).

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With Other Users: Your public profile (name, age, job/school, favorite movies/shows, and photos) is visible to other CineSpace users to facilitate matching.
  • Service Providers: We use trusted third-party services:
    • Google Firebase: For backend hosting, authentication, and database services.
    • RevenueCat: For managing in-app subscriptions and purchases.
    • TMDB (The Movie Database): To fetch movie and TV show metadata (Note: We do not send your personal data to TMDB).
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

4. User Generated Content (UGC) & Safety

CineSpace is committed to maintaining a safe and respectful community.

  • Zero Tolerance Policy: We have zero tolerance for objectionable content, harassment, hate speech, or abusive behavior.
  • Reporting & Blocking: Users can Report inappropriate content/profiles or Block abusive users directly within the App via the profile menu or chat screen.
  • Action: Reported content is reviewed within 24 hours. Validated violations will result in content removal and/or immediate account suspension/ban.

5. Data Deletion (Account Deletion)

You have full control over your data. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • Go to Settings within the App.
  • Select Delete Account.
  • Confirm the action.

Once confirmed, your profile, matches, chat history, and favorite lists will be permanently deleted from our servers. This action is irreversible.

6. Age Restriction

CineSpace is not intended for use by persons under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18. If we become aware that a user is under 18, we will take steps to delete their information and terminate the account.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
